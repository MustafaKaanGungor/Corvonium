What the drawing settles

Confirmed

Three bricks, then "+2 more"

At real dimensions a day cell is 92 px. The day number takes 22, each brick 17. Three fit. The ceiling the plan predicted is exactly the ceiling the grid has.

Week segmentation

Family visit, cut in half

13 – 18 August crosses a row boundary, so it draws twice — squared off with a chevron at each cut. Two segments, one item, fixed lane on both rows.

Plan vs Effort

The toggle earns the cell

Because Effort replaces the bricks rather than joining them, intensity gets the full 92 px — a proportional bar and an hour figure, instead of the few spare pixels a combined view would have left.

Derived, not stored

The matrix sorts itself

Nothing on the task screen carries an urgency flag. "Send the invoice" sits in Urgent & Important because it is due at 17:00 today, and it will leave on its own tomorrow.

Capture

The parser shows its work

One sentence in, a filled-in item out. Words the parser used stay underlined where you typed them, and every value it extracted is a chip — so a wrong read is visible, and one tap puts the words back.

Two rows, two axes

Time above, project below

Splitting the filters matches the model: one narrows by when, the other by which project, and they combine. A single scrolling strip hid that they were independent.
